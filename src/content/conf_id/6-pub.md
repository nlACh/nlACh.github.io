---
title: "Au-NP induced defect engineering of bilayerHfO2/p-Si MIS capacitors for controlled charge trapping"
authors: "N. Paul, A. Mukherjee, S. Chattopadhyay"
event: "(Accepted) IEEE EDKCON 2026"
abstract: "Semiconductor and metal nanocrystal mediated defect control in dielectric stacks presents a promising route for developing memory devices using MOSFETs. In this context, current work studies the impact of gold nanoparticle incorporation in bilayer-HfO2 based MIS capacitors. It is observed that Au-NP incorporation increases the leakage current by ~100 times, along with a ‘butterfly’ shaped hysteresis. The loop C-V characteristics of the devices exhibit hysteresis in accumulation region. For the bilayer device, the CV characteristics indicate presence of positive charges in the stack and trap states that are active in both forward and reverse sweeps. Au-NP incorporation in such a stack introduces more positive charges into the stack, along with significant dispersion in the reverse bias sweep. The C-V plots further suggest the accumulation of higher quantity of charge at the HfO2/Si interface (from ~200 nC/cm^2 to ~325 nC/cm^2) due to Au-NP incorporation into the stack."
